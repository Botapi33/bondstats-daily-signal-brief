Signal Engine v2

Original by architecture.

The Daily Signal Brief is designed to detect changes in structured financial data, not to reproduce the editorial work of financial-news organizations.

What enters the engine

Structured BondStats market feeds and factual observations sourced from official institutions. Current inputs include sovereign-yield observations, the BondStats Macro Data Watch, Central Bank Watch and Market Calendar. Official source links remain attached where applicable.

What never enters the engine

  • Third-party news articles or article bodies.
  • Copied or rewritten news headlines.
  • Publisher screenshots, charts, logos or branded graphics.
  • Unlicensed consensus forecasts.
  • Invented explanations for coincident market moves.
The engine can say that two verified signals diverge. It cannot say one caused the other unless that causal claim is independently established.

Daily-change integrity

A sovereign-yield observation can enter the daily rates tape only if the upstream record is daily, non-fallback and no more than seven days stale, with both current and previous values available. Monthly or stale observations are never converted into fake daily moves.

Signal scores

Signal scores are transparent prioritization heuristics, not probabilities, forecasts or investment recommendations. Market-move magnitude, breadth, source freshness, event proximity and archive rarity can raise a score. A score does not imply expected returns.

Signal memory

Each day’s first successful snapshot is immutable. Historical state recurrence, streaks and market-move percentiles are computed only from this BondStats archive. The memory layer therefore becomes more informative as the archive grows.

Source failure

If an upstream fetch fails, the engine may use a last-known-good snapshot and marks that source degraded. If neither a live source nor a usable snapshot exists, the source is marked unavailable. Missing data is not silently invented.

Copyright and source rights

The BondStats interface, deterministic narrative templates, signal logic, curation and original presentation are BondStats work. Public facts and source data remain subject to the rights and terms of their respective providers. This design reduces exposure to third-party editorial copyright by not ingesting or rewriting news content; it is not a blanket legal declaration that every underlying data point is “copyright free.”
