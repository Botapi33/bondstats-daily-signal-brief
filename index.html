CHECKING

Built to be original.

BondStats does not ingest, scrape, copy, summarize or rewrite third-party news articles, headlines, screenshots or editorial text for this brief. The prose is generated deterministically from structured facts, official-source observations and BondStats data feeds.

This architecture is designed to reduce copyright and licensing exposure. Underlying source terms still apply to source data; BondStats does not claim ownership of public facts supplied by third parties.

Daily memory, not daily noise.

Each successful day creates an immutable dated snapshot. Re-running the workflow can refresh today’s live brief, but it cannot silently rewrite the archived historical record for that date.
